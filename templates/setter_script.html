Setter Instructions

Your Job Is To Book The Appointment (Nothing Else)

⚠️ Important: You are NOT doing discovery and NOT pitching/closing. Those happen on the call with me. Your only goal: get them curious enough to book a time, then confirm they'll actually show up.
The Process
  1. During the day: scrape leads
  2. That night: send each lead a text/DM
  3. Next day: call them, referencing the text
Step 1 — Opener

"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]. I sent you a message [yesterday/this morning] — were you able to see it?"

If yes: skip to Step 2.

If no:

"No worries — quick rundown: we're calling because a lot of contractors either want more jobs but don't know who to trust, or they've tried to get more work and gotten scammed. We're looking for contractors who want to grow but just need the right guidance. Does that sound like you at all?"

If they say "not really" — thank them and move on. Don't push.
Step 2 — Spark Curiosity (keep it short, don't explain how it works)

"Yeah, honestly — we've worked with a [their trade] company having that same issue, and we were able to help them fix it. I'm not here to pitch you or convince you of anything today — I just want to get you on a quick call with my partner to show you the results and see if it's even a fit. If not, no worries at all."

DO NOT get into:
  • Specifics of how it works
  • Pricing
  • The offer details

If they ask detailed questions:

"Great question — that's exactly what we'll cover on the call, I don't want to give you half the picture."

Step 3 — Book The Time

"Is that something you'd be open to jumping on a quick call for?"

If yes: Lock in a specific day/time. Don't be vague.

If no: Move on to the next lead. No negotiating.

Step 4 — No-Show Filter (say this EVERY time, right after booking)

"One thing — you know how it goes when someone books an estimate and then doesn't show or cancels last minute? I want to avoid wasting your time and ours. So be honest with me — are you actually going to be there [day/time]? If not, totally fine to say so now."

Why this matters: This filters out people who won't show. You're giving them a guilt-free way to say no NOW instead of ghosting.
After Each Call — Log This
  • ✓ Did they book? (yes/no)
  • ✓ Date & time confirmed
  • ✓ Call status (not contacted, attempted, connected, converted, declined)
  • ✓ Any notes from the conversation
  • ✓ Did they pass the no-show filter?

🎯 Key Mindset

You're not selling anything. You're just getting them curious enough to take a call.

No means no. If they say no or "not really," thank them and hang up. Don't negotiate or overcome objections.

Specificity matters. Always lock in a specific day/time. "Let me know" = wasted effort.

The filter question is your friend. Better they say no now than ghost you later.

Your job ends when they book. Everything else is my job on the call.
